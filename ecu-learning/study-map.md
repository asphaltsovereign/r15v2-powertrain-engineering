# Engine-management study map

Status: learning in progress. These are independently written notes; they are not a reproduction of Techmaghi course material and do not establish course completion or professional tuning competence.

| Topic | Engineering question | Evidence to produce |
|---|---|---|
| Load estimation | What do MAF, MAP and throttle information measure, and what is assumed? | Explain speed-density and alpha-N with units and assumptions |
| Fuel delivery | How does pulse width relate to cycle time and injector capacity? | A calculation using independently verified engine/injector data |
| Ignition | Why do load, speed, combustion and knock limits matter? | A source-linked conceptual explanation, no vehicle calibration prescription |
| Measurement quality | What is a reference, a calibration error or a missing signal? | Bench comparison and uncertainty record |
| Diagnostics | What can the actual available channels support? | An observation-to-hypothesis table with explicit exclusions |

Study progress, acquired hardware and successful testing are separate claims. The next useful proof is a short technical explanation and a reproducible measurement, not a copied certificate or an unverified tune file.
